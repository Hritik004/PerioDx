From the build log

Why a bone-loss score alone wasn't enough

A raw percentage doesn't tell a patient what to do next, so PerioDx is being built as a deep learning-based system that detects periodontitis and estimates bone loss from dental X-ray images, then explains the result rather than just reporting it.

Detection happens in stages: a YOLO-based segmentation model localizes each tooth and its surrounding bone to compute % bone loss, and medical image processing techniques prepare the raw radiograph for reliable input. On top of that sits a small language model (SLM) that turns the score into a personalized answer a patient can actually understand — this piece is still ongoing.
